# Competitor Profiles & Landscape Teardowns

This reference guide provides verified technical and business profiles of major competitive platforms evaluated in the ACDP research.

---

## 1. Devpost (Global Hackathon Platform)
- **Primary Audience**: US/European software engineers, corporate hackathon sponsors.
- **Ingestion**: 100% manual listing submitted by hackathon organizers.
- **Search & Discovery**: Keyword tags and location filters; no conversational rulebook Q&A.
- **Team Matching**: Basic public message board where participants post self-introductions; no skill complementarity algorithm.
- **Faculty / Academic Integration**: Completely absent.
- **Coverage in Vietnam**: $< 5\%$ of local Vietnamese university contests.

---

## 2. Unstop (formerly Dare2Compete)
- **Primary Audience**: Indian engineering and business school competitions.
- **Ingestion**: Paid / sponsored organizer portal.
- **Search & Discovery**: Tabular listings with deadline dates; static rule documents.
- **Team Matching**: Search by user profile, but without semantic vector matching.
- **Institutional Analytics**: High-cost enterprise tier for universities; not open-source.
- **Coverage in Vietnam**: $< 2\%$ coverage.

---

## 3. Vietnamese Status Quo (Facebook & Youth Union Channels)
- **Channels**: Faculty Fanpages (FIT HCMUTE, Đoàn Khoa), student groups, Youth Union (*Đoàn - Hội*), third-party aggregators (Ybox, TopCV).
- **Format**: Unstructured JPEG flyers, status update captions, Google Forms links.
- **Bottlenecks**: Deadlines frequently missed due to algorithmic social media feeds; search is virtually impossible after a few weeks; no central historical repository of winning solutions.
